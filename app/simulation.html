Hardware assumptions & fault injection

Configured pulses are assumed calibrated. The plant assumes 12 ms contact bounce and motor motion stops when PWM is detached. Real SG90 current, holding force, binding, supply sag and brownouts are not calculated. PWM removal on real hardware does not guarantee motor power cutoff.

Event trace
    What is running, and what is modeled?

    Purchased hardware: ESP-WROOM-32, SG90, tactile buttons and LM2596 buck converter. The model also assumes two endpoint switches and an external LED, absent from the purchase list. LM2596 requires a DC source above the 5 V output with sufficient headroom; a single 18650 cell cannot supply this circuit through it.
